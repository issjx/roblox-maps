# Demo Obby — design

**Pitch:** a short climb over lava. 12 platforms, 3 checkpoints, about 1 minute.

**Audience:** new players, ages 8+. Difficulty: easy.

**Core loop:** jump → reach a checkpoint → stage counter goes up → fall = lava = respawn at the last checkpoint.

**Stages**
| Stage | Platforms | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | P001–P004 | Warm-up, gentle zigzag |
| 2 | P005–P008 | Same rhythm, higher up |
| 3 | P009–P012 | Finish line |

**Mechanics used:** platforms, checkpoints, lava (all shared — no new scripts).

**Store page:** title "Demo Obby", description as in map.json, thumbnail: yellow platforms climbing over glowing lava at sunset.
